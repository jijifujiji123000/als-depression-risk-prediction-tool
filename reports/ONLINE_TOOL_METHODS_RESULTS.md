# Manuscript text for the online prediction tool

## Methods

An English-language web-based risk calculator was developed to facilitate research use of the locked 3- and 6-month prediction models. For each prediction horizon, the interface applies 20 imputation-specific model pipelines and reports the arithmetic mean of the 20 predicted probabilities. The 3-month calculator uses the locked logistic-regression models and a prespecified classification threshold of 0.35, whereas the 6-month calculator uses the locked XGBoost models and a threshold of 0.28. Neutrophil-to-lymphocyte ratio (NLR) is calculated automatically from the entered absolute neutrophil and lymphocyte counts, after which log1p(NLR) is supplied to the corresponding model. The online implementation contains the original development-cohort standardization parameters and locked model parameters and performs no online training, variable selection, hyperparameter tuning, threshold optimization, or recalibration. All calculations are performed locally in the user's browser; no identifying information is requested, and entered values are not transmitted to a prediction server, stored, or logged. The calculator is available at https://jijifujiji123000.github.io/als-depression-risk-prediction-tool/.

## Results

The web application provides separate 3- and 6-month interfaces, displays the required baseline variables and units, automatically calculates NLR and log1p(NLR), and returns the model-predicted probability together with the fixed classification threshold and risk category. In the temporal validation cohort, the locked 3-month logistic-regression model achieved an AUROC of 0.729, an area under the precision-recall curve of 0.614, and a Brier score of 0.178; at the prespecified threshold of 0.35, accuracy, sensitivity, specificity, positive predictive value, and F1 score were 0.726, 0.490, 0.829, 0.556, and 0.521, respectively. The locked 6-month XGBoost model achieved an AUROC of 0.768, an area under the precision-recall curve of 0.675, and a Brier score of 0.201; at the threshold of 0.28, the corresponding values were 0.699, 0.593, 0.763, 0.603, and 0.598. Conversion of the locked models to the browser-executable format was verified against the original Python pipelines across 4,000 model-level synthetic predictions, with a maximum absolute probability difference of 1.70 x 10^-7. The original Streamlit implementation additionally passed all four prespecified automated reproduction tests.
