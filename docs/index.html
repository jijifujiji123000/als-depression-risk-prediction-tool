ALS Depressive-Symptom Risk Prediction Tool

Baseline estimation of 3- and 6-month risk

This English-language research tool applies the formally locked models without online fitting, tuning, or recalibration. It estimates the probability of PHQ-9-defined depressive symptoms and does not provide a clinical diagnosis.

Locked analysisTwenty imputation-specific models are evaluated for each prediction horizon.
Fixed classification rulesPrespecified thresholds are 0.35 at 3 months and 0.28 at 6 months.
Browser-only calculationInputs remain on this device and are not transmitted, stored, or logged.

Research-use notice

This tool is intended for academic demonstration and research use. Predicted risk should be interpreted together with validated symptom scales, clinical interviews, and professional assessment. It must not be used as the sole basis for diagnosis or treatment.

No names, identifiers, hospital numbers, contact details, or other personal information should be entered.
